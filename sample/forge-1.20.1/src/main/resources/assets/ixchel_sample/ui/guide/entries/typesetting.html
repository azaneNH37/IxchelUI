Text in a display: block box flows across spans, colors, links and items like in one paragraph, and breaks between words when a line is full.
Whitespace collapses: line breaks and indentation in the template read as single spaces, and a space between two elements stays.
中文没有空格，行内在任意两个汉字之间都可以断开。句号、逗号和右括号不会出现在行首，左括号不会留在行尾：「这样」排出来的段落读起来才顺。
Mixed text works too: 红石粉 可以传递 15 格信号，每经过一格减弱一级。
Bold, italic, struck and underlined links come from font-weight, font-style and text-decoration; nested spans add to each other.
Keys such as Ctrl + C are inline-block boxes: they keep a background and padding and never split across lines.
A word longer than the line, such as supercalifragilisticexpialidocious, is cut where it overflows, since there is nowhere else to break it.
