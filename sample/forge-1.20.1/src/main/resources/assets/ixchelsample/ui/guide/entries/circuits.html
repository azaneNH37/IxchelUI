Redstone dust carries a signal up to 15 blocks; each block it crosses weakens it by one.
A redstone torch is a power source that turns off when the block it stands on is powered.
A piston pushes up to twelve blocks when powered.
A redstone lamp lights up while powered and goes dark two ticks after the signal ends.
A repeater restores a signal to full strength and delays it by one to four ticks; right-click it to change the delay.
Signals do not pass through a repeater backwards: it is a diode. Keep the parts of a build in storage nearby.
